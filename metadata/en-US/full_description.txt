ORIOUS is a free, secure, and open-source Android app to manage your two-factor authentication tokens, passwords, and private notes inside one encrypted offline vault.

<b>Three integrated vault sections</b>
<ul><li><b>Authenticator</b>: Supports HOTP, TOTP, Steam, mOTP, and YAOTP tokens, QR scanning, clipboard import, tap-to-reveal codes, custom icons, and groups.</li><li><b>Password Manager</b>: Save credentials with a built-in configurable password generator, quick copy actions, and search.</li><li><b>Private Notebook</b>: Write and organize multiline personal notes with a distraction-free editor, favorites, sorting, search, and delete confirmation.</li></ul>

<b>Encryption and biometric unlock</b>
All 2FA secrets, passwords, and private notes are stored inside a single AES-256-GCM encrypted vault file. Unlock with your password or Android Keystore biometrics, with screen capture prevention and automatic locking.

<b>Encrypted backups and imports</b>
Create automatic or manual encrypted backups containing your entire vault (2FA entries, passwords, and private notes), or import from 2FAS, andOTP, Authenticator Plus, Authy, Bitwarden, Ente Auth, FreeOTP/FreeOTP+, Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Proton Authenticator, Steam, Stratum, and WinAuth.
